🎪 SMART SNAKE — GAME INSTRUCTIONS

Welcome to Smart Snake! Read the instructions before playing.

  • After closing these notes, enter your name and course / section and choose Player vs AI or Solo Run.
  • Press the physical DOWN button to save your registration.
  • After saving, press the physical START button to begin the game.
  • UP, DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT buttons: control your snake while playing.
  • Keyboard controls: Arrow keys or W, A, S, D can also move your snake.
  • SPACE: pause or resume the game.
  • Food & Boosts: Apple +10, Berry +20, Orange +30, Golden coin +50, Speed boost, and AI KO +100.
  • Block obstacles: avoid walls, yourself, the AI snake, and red brick blocks.
  • You have 60 seconds to get the highest score.
